## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-17

### Added

- Hardware lighting modes, per-key RGB and persistent custom lighting.
- Live animation engine and bundled effects.
- Music visualiser modes driven by CAVA.
- Hyprland keybind HUD.
- Read-back verification for settings written to the keyboard.
- Linux udev rule and systemd service examples.
- Automated tests for colour conversion and frame serialisation.
